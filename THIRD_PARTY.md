## Imported workflows

The `multi-role-repo-review`, `platform-documentation`, and `technical-debt-auditor` packages, and the production collection — eleven specialist lanes and `production-pass-orchestrator`, plus the separately authored `production-readiness-report` — come from the maintainer's existing local workflow collection, adapted to this playbook's testing, delegation, and authority policies. Their original authorship was not recorded. Their inclusion is not a claim that they were first written for this repository.
